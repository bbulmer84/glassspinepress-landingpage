Care Before Clarity

“This is the best book I’ve read in that it has a very different storyline. It has detailed and masterful language.

The characterisation captures the struggle of the individual dealing with an enemy he can’t control. An enemy others can’t even see coming.

The author is building a true sense of menace. I can’t wait to read the next book.”

— Early Reader
